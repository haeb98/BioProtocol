Title: Isolation of Chromatin-bound Proteins from Subcellular Fractions for Biochemical Analysis

1. Cell Culture and Maintenance
   1.1. Grow Cor3–1 neural stem cells in Complete media.
   1.2. Supplement Complete media with 10 ng/ml mouse EGF, 10 ng/ml human FGF, and 1 μg/ml Laminin before changing the medium or passaging the cells.
   1.3. Maintain P19 cells in MEM-α supplemented with 10% FBS, 100 units/ml penicillin, and 100 μg/ml streptomycin.

2. Neuronal Differentiation
   2.1. 24 hours after seeding Cor3–1 cells, transfer them into priming medium Euromed-N supplemented with 1% B-27 without vitamin A, 0.5% N2, and 10 ng/ml human FGF for 3 days.
   2.2. After 3 days in priming medium, transfer cells into differentiation medium (1:3 ratio DMEM/HAM’S-F12:Neurobasal) supplemented with 1% B-27 without vitamin A, 0.5% N2, 10 ng/ml human FGF, 20 ng/ml BDNF, and 1μg/ml Laminin.
   2.3. Change the differentiation medium every 3 days, reducing the FGF concentration by half each time until it reaches zero, while maintaining a constant concentration of BDNF at 30 ng/ml.

3. Cell Transfection
   3.1. Grow P19 cells to 80% confluence in MEM-α medium supplemented with 10% FBS, 100 units/ml penicillin, and 100 μg/ml streptomycin.
   3.2. Prepare the transfection mixture using Lipofectamine 2000 and plasmid DNA at a 1:2 ratio in proliferation medium.
   3.3. Transfect P19 cells at 80% confluence with the prepared transfection mixture.

4. Cellular Fractionation
   4.1. Wash Cor3–1 and P19 cells once with room temperature PBS and scrape into 1 ml PBS.
   4.2. Pellet cells by centrifugation at 1,200 rpm for 3 min at 4°C.
   4.3. Resuspend cell pellets in 5 volumes of ice-cold E1 buffer supplemented with 1× protease inhibitor cocktail and gently pipette up and down 5 times.
   4.4. Centrifuge at 3,400 rpm for 2 min at 4°C and collect the supernatant as the cytoplasm fraction.
   4.5. Resuspend the pellet in the same volume of E1 buffer, centrifuge as before, and discard the supernatant.
   4.6. Resuspend the pellet in 2 volumes of ice-cold E2 buffer supplemented with 1× protease inhibitor cocktail, leave for 10 min on ice, centrifuge, and collect the supernatant as the nucleus fraction.
   4.7. Resuspend the remaining pellet in the same volume of E3 buffer supplemented with 1× protease inhibitor cocktail, sonicate for 5 min on setting H (30 sec ON/30 sec OFF), and centrifuge at 13,000 rpm for 10 min at 4°C to obtain the chromatin fraction.
   4.8. Determine the protein concentration of all fractions using the BCA protein assay kit.

5. Agarose-TUBEs Pull Down
   5.1. Incubate 15 μl of 50% Agarose-TUBEs with 350-450 μg of ubiquitylated proteins from the cytoplasm and chromatin fractions in 500 μl final volume of respective buffers for 1 hour at 4°C on a rocker platform.
   5.2. Centrifuge Agarose-TUBEs at 1,000 × g for 5 min at 4°C and collect the supernatant as the unbound fraction.
   5.3. Wash Agarose-TUBEs 3 times according to the manufacturer’s instructions.
   5.4. Boil Agarose-TUBEs in reducing sample buffer for 5 min, centrifuge at maximum speed for 1 min, and load the eluted samples onto gels alongside 5 μg of proteins from the unbound fraction and 5 μg from fractionated lysates (Input).

6. Western Blotting
   6.1. Lyse cells directly with RIPA buffer supplemented with protease inhibitor cocktail or use fractionated lysates.
   6.2. Determine protein concentration using the BCA protein assay kit.
   6.3. Carry out SDS-PAGE electrophoresis using 10% pre-cast polyacrylamide gels in MOPS buffer.
   6.4. Transfer separated proteins onto nitrocellulose membranes.
   6.5. Block membranes with 10% non-fat milk in TBS-0.1% Tween solution for 1 hour.
   6.6. Probe membranes overnight at 4°C with primary antibodies specific to the proteins of interest.
   6.7. Develop membranes using ECL Prime for Ascl1 or ECL detection reagents for other antibodies.

7. Protein Stability Assay
   7.1. Treat Cor3-1 cells or transfected P19 cells with 10 μg/ml cycloheximide.
   7.2. At defined time points, harvest cells with RIPA lysis buffer or fractionate as described above.
   7.3. Separate equal amounts of each whole cell lysate or fractionated lysate on SDS-PAGE and transfer to a membrane for immunoblotting.
   7.4. Quantify protein levels using FiJi software and calculate half-lives using nonlinear regression analysis (One phase decay) with Prism software.

Please note that this protocol is a summary and should be performed in conjunction with the detailed methods and conditions provided in the original experimental descriptions.
