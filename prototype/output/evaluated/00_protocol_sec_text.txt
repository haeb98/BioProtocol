Title: Isolation of Chromatin-bound Proteins from Subcellular Fractions for Biochemical Analysis

Materials:
- Cor3–1 and P19 cells
- Complete media components (as listed above)
- Priming medium components (as listed above)
- Differentiation medium components (as listed above)
- Adenovirus CMV-CRE and CMV-null (Vector Biolabs, 1045 N)
- Lipofectamine 2000 (Invitrogen, 11668-027)
- Plasmids (as listed in Supplementary Table S2)
- PBS (Phosphate Buffered Saline)
- E1, E2, and E3 buffers (composition as listed above)
- Protease inhibitor cocktail (cOmplete, Roche)
- Bioruptor (Diagenode)
- Benzonase (Novagen, 70664-3)
- BCA protein assay kit (Thermo)
- Agarose-TUBEs (LifeSensors, UM401)
- RIPA lysis buffer (Sigma-Aldrich, R0278)
- Cycloheximide (Sigma-Aldrich, C4859)
- SDS-PAGE and Western blotting materials
- Antibodies (as listed above)
- ECL detection reagents (GE Healthcare, RPN2106 and RPN2232)
- Centrifuge and microcentrifuge
- Rocker platform

Procedure:

1. Cell Culture and Transfection:
   1.1. Grow Cor3–1 and P19 cells in their respective media as described above.
   1.2. For neuronal differentiation of Cor3–1 cells, follow the medium change and supplementation schedule as specified.
   1.3. Transduce Floxed-Huwe1-RYFP Cor3–1 cells with adenovirus CMV-CRE or CMV-null as controls.
   1.4. Transfect P19 cells with Ascl1 plasmid constructs using Lipofectamine 2000 when cells reach 80% confluence.

2. Cellular Fractionation:
   2.1. Seed Cor3–1 cells at 1.5 × 10^6 in 10 cm dishes and P19 cells at 1.0 × 10^6 in 10 cm dishes.
   2.2. Wash cells with room temperature PBS and scrape into 1 ml PBS.
   2.3. Pellet cells at 1,200 rpm at 4°C for 3 min.
   2.4. Resuspend pellets in 5 volumes of ice-cold E1 buffer with protease inhibitors and pipette gently 5 times.
   2.5. Centrifuge at 3,400 rpm at 4°C for 2 min and collect the supernatant (cytoplasmic fraction).
   2.6. Resuspend the pellet in the same volume of E1 buffer and repeat centrifugation. Discard the supernatant.
   2.7. Resuspend the pellet in 2 volumes of ice-cold E2 buffer with protease inhibitors and centrifuge. Collect the supernatant (nuclear fraction).
   2.8. Resuspend the remaining pellet in ice-cold E3 buffer with protease inhibitors, sonicate for 5 min (Bioruptor, 30 sec ON/30 sec OFF), and centrifuge. Collect the supernatant (chromatin fraction).

3. Protein Quantification:
   3.1. Determine protein concentration of each fraction using the BCA protein assay kit.

4. Agarose-TUBEs Pull Down:
   4.1. Incubate 15 μl of 50% Agarose-TUBEs with 350-450 μg of proteins from the cytoplasmic and chromatin fractions for 1 hour at 4°C on a rocker platform.
   4.2. Centrifuge at 1,000 × g at 4°C for 5 min and collect the supernatant (unbound fraction).
   4.3. Wash the Agarose-TUBEs 3 times as per the manufacturer’s instructions.
   4.4. Boil the Agarose-TUBEs in reducing sample buffer for 5 min, centrifuge, and collect the eluted samples (bound fraction).

5. Western Blot Analysis:
   5.1. Prepare samples from total cell lysates or fractionated lysates.
   5.2. Perform SDS-PAGE and transfer proteins onto nitrocellulose membranes.
   5.3. Block membranes with 10% non-fat milk in TBS-0.1% Tween and probe overnight with primary antibodies.
   5.4. Incubate with appropriate HRP-conjugated secondary antibodies.
   5.5. Develop membranes using ECL detection reagents.

6. Data Analysis:
   6.1. Quantify protein levels using densitometry software.
   6.2. Analyze data and present as means ± SEM from at least three independent experiments.

Note: Ensure to maintain sterile conditions throughout the cell culture process and handle all samples on ice to prevent protein degradation. Dispose of waste according to your institution's safety protocols.
