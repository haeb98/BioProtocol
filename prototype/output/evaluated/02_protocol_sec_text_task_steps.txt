Title: Isolation of Chromatin-bound Proteins from Subcellular Fractions for Biochemical Analysis

Materials:
- Cell culture dishes (10 cm)
- PBS (Phosphate-Buffered Saline), room temperature
- Eppendorf tubes
- Ice-cold E1 buffer (50 mM Hepes-KOH pH 7.5, 140 mM NaCl, 1 mM EDTA pH 8.0, 10% glycerol, 0.5% NP-40, 0.25% Triton X-100, 1 mM DTT)
- Protease inhibitor cocktail (1×)
- Ice-cold E2 buffer (10 mM Tris-HCl pH 8.0, 200 mM NaCl, 1 mM EDTA pH 8.0, 0.5 mM EGTA pH 8.0)
- Ice-cold E3 buffer (500 mM Tris-HCl, 500 mM NaCl)
- Bioruptor (Diagenode) or equivalent sonicator
- Centrifuge capable of 13,000 rpm at 4°C
- BCA protein assay kit (Thermo)
- Benzonase (1:1,000, Novagen, 70664-3) for TUBEs pull down (optional)

Methods:
1. Seed Cor3–1 cells at 1.5 × 10^6 and P19 cells at 1.0 × 10^6 in 10 cm dishes. If P19 cells are to be transfected, use 3 μg of Ascl1 plasmid constructs (see Supplementary Table S2).

2. Once cells have reached the desired confluency, wash them once with room temperature PBS and scrape them into 1 ml PBS.

3. Pellet cells by centrifugation at 1,200 rpm in an Eppendorf tube at 4°C for 3 minutes.

4. Resuspend the cell pellet in 5 volumes of ice-cold E1 buffer complemented with 1× protease inhibitor cocktail. Pipette gently up and down 5 times.

5. Centrifuge at 3,400 rpm at 4°C for 2 minutes. Collect the supernatant in a fresh tube (cytoplasm fraction).

6. Resuspend the pellet in the same volume of E1 buffer and repeat the centrifugation. Discard the supernatant.

7. Resuspend the pellet in the same volume of E1 buffer and leave for 10 minutes on ice.

8. Centrifuge at 3,400 rpm at 4°C for 2 minutes. Discard the supernatant.

9. Resuspend the pellet in 2 volumes of ice-cold E2 buffer complemented with 1× protease inhibitor cocktail. Centrifuge at 3,400 rpm at 4°C for 2 minutes. Collect the supernatant in a fresh tube (nucleus fraction).

10. Resuspend the pellet in the same volume of E2 buffer and repeat centrifugation. Discard the supernatant.

11. Resuspend the pellet in ice-cold E3 buffer complemented with 1× protease inhibitor cocktail by gentle pipetting in the same volume used for E1.

12. Sonicate the solution (chromatin fraction) in a Bioruptor for 5 minutes on setting H, 30 seconds ON/30 seconds OFF.

13. Centrifuge the sonicated solution at 13,000 rpm at 4°C for 10 minutes. The supernatant contains the chromatin-bound proteins.

14. Determine the protein concentration of all fractions using the BCA protein assay kit.

15. Proceed with downstream applications such as western blot analysis or TUBEs pull down. If performing TUBEs pull down, optionally digest the chromatin fraction with Benzonase (1:1,000) before the pull down.

Note: Ensure that all steps involving cell lysates and fractions are performed on ice or at 4°C to prevent protein degradation. Use protease inhibitors throughout the process to protect the proteins from proteolytic activity.
