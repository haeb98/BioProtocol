Title: Isolation of Chromatin-bound Proteins from Subcellular Fractions for Biochemical Analysis

Materials:
- Cor3–1 neural stem cells
- P19 cells
- Complete media with growth factors
- MEM-α medium
- Fetal bovine serum (FBS)
- Antibiotics (penicillin and streptomycin)
- Priming medium
- Differentiation medium
- Fibroblast growth factor (FGF)
- Brain-derived neurotrophic factor (BDNF)
- Plasmid DNA
- Lipofectamine 2000
- RNeasy kit (Qiagen)
- QuantiTect Reverse Transcription Kit (Qiagen)
- QuantiFast SYBR Green Master Mix (Qiagen)
- Lightcycler 480 PCR system (Roche)
- RIPA buffer
- Protease inhibitor cocktail
- BCA protein assay kit
- SDS-PAGE electrophoresis materials
- Nitrocellulose membranes
- ECL Prime and ECL detection reagents
- PBS
- E1, E2, and E3 buffers
- Sonicator
- Agarose-TUBEs
- Cycloheximide
- FiJi software
- Prism software
- Paraformaldehyde
- Triton X-100
- BSA
- Anti-Ascl1 primary antibody
- Alexa488 goat anti-rabbit IgG secondary antibody
- DAPI
- Axio Scan (Zeiss)
- ΔM short-Luciferase, pRL-TK (Renilla, Promega)
- Dual-Luciferase Reporter Assay System

Methods:

Cell Culture and Maintenance:
1. Grow Cor3–1 neural stem cells in Complete media.
2. Before changing the medium or passaging, supplement Complete media with growth factors.
3. Maintain P19 cells in MEM-α supplemented with 10% FBS and antibiotics (100 units/ml penicillin and 100 μg/ml streptomycin).

Neuronal Differentiation:
4. 24 hours after seeding Cor3–1 cells, transfer them into priming medium.
5. Maintain cells in priming medium for 3 days.
6. After the priming period, transfer cells into differentiation medium.
7. Change the differentiation medium every 3 days, halving the FGF concentration each time until it is eliminated, while maintaining a constant BDNF concentration of 30 ng/ml.

Cell Transfection:
8. Grow P19 cells in MEM-α medium supplemented with 10% FBS and antibiotics until 80% confluence.
9. Prepare transfection mixture by diluting plasmid DNA and Lipofectamine 2000 in the proliferation medium at a 1:2 ratio.
10. Transfect P19 cells at 80% confluence with the mixture, following the manufacturer's instructions for Lipofectamine 2000.

Cellular Fractionation:
11. Wash cells once with room temperature PBS and scrape into 1 ml PBS.
12. Pellet cells by centrifugation at 1,200 rpm at 4°C for 3 minutes.
13. Resuspend the cell pellet in 5 volumes of ice-cold E1 buffer with 1x protease inhibitor cocktail and pipette gently 5 times.
14. Centrifuge at 3,400 rpm at 4°C for 2 minutes and collect the supernatant (cytoplasm fraction).
15. Resuspend the pellet in the same volume of E1 buffer, centrifuge as before, and discard the supernatant.
16. Resuspend the pellet in the same volume of E2 buffer with 1x protease inhibitor cocktail, leave for 10 minutes on ice, centrifuge, and collect the supernatant (nucleus fraction).
17. Resuspend the pellet in the same volume of E3 buffer with 1x protease inhibitor cocktail, sonicate for 5 minutes (30 seconds ON/30 seconds OFF), and centrifuge at 13,000 rpm at 4°C for 10 minutes to obtain the chromatin fraction.
18. Determine the protein concentration of all fractions using the BCA protein assay kit.

Note: The remaining tasks (Quantitative Real-Time PCR, Western blotting, Agarose-TUBEs pull down, Protein stability assay, Immunocytochemistry, and Gene reporter assay) are not directly related to the isolation of chromatin-bound proteins but are subsequent analyses that can be performed using the isolated proteins. If these analyses are to be included in the protocol, please provide additional instructions.
